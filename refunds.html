Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

1. Before payment

You can leave the quiz or close the website at any time before payment without being charged.

2. After successful payment

The ₹49 payment is for creation and delivery of a personalized career roadmap. Because the report generation process starts after successful payment, refund requests are handled as described below.

3. Eligible refund situations

If you were charged successfully but the personalized report could not be generated or delivered because of a technical failure on our side, contact us and we will either resolve delivery or issue a refund for that transaction.

4. Duplicate or erroneous charges

If you believe you were charged twice for the same purchase, contact us with the transaction details. We will review the records and refund any confirmed duplicate charge.

5. Non-refundable situations

Once the personalized report has been successfully generated and made available to you, a refund is generally not available merely because you did not like a recommendation or changed your mind. We may consider exceptional cases at our discretion where applicable law requires otherwise.

6. How to request help

Email help@meriapniudaan.in with your payment reference, date and a brief description of the issue.

7. Processing

Approved refunds are returned through the applicable payment channel used for the transaction and may take additional banking/payment-network time to reflect.
