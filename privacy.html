Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Meri Apni Udaan (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, career quiz and personalized career report service.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the information you provide through the quiz, such as education level, career goals, interests, work preferences, location, current situation, time availability and other answers. We may also collect contact information such as your name and email address when required to deliver your report or support you.

2. How we use it

We use your information to calculate career-fit recommendations, personalize your career roadmap, identify relevant opportunity sources, process payments, generate and deliver your report, provide support and improve the service.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by our payment provider, Razorpay. We do not ask you to share payment credentials such as UPI PIN, card PIN or banking passwords with us.

4. Location and opportunity information

Your location may be used to tailor state, city or nearby opportunity sources. Vacancy information can change; official sources should always be checked before applying.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, support, legal, accounting and security purposes.

6. Your choices

You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and records we are required to retain.

7. Contact

For privacy questions: help@meriapniudaan.in
