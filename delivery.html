Delivery Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Meri Apni Udaan provides a digital personalized career roadmap. No physical product is shipped.

1. How delivery works

After successful payment, your personalized career roadmap is generated from your quiz responses. The report is made available as a downloadable PDF on the website.

2. Email delivery

A copy of the report may also be sent to the email address you provide during the purchase or delivery process.

3. Delivery timing

We aim to make the report available shortly after successful payment. In the event of a technical delay, please contact us at help@meriapniudaan.in.

4. Failed delivery

If you have completed payment but cannot access your report, contact us with your payment reference. We will investigate the issue and restore access or process a refund where applicable under our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
